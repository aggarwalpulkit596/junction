Junction

Route-building rail games for your phone, with friends or robots

Drive the Delhi Metro. Everyone at once. Three minutes a round.

Junction: Delhi Metro is a real-time route-building game made for phones. Passes arrive every few seconds, extra ones float above stations for the first train there, and you tap a segment to drive it the moment you can pay. Three rounds of three minutes on a 3D toy Delhi, with signal failures and jams. Play solo against three robots, or online with up to 4 friends.
